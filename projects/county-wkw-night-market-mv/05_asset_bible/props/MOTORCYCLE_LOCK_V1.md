Status: V1 candidate image lock generated.

## Visual Identity

The motorcycle is an ordinary used county-town street bike or retro scooter. It is emotional hardware, not a luxury object. Wet chrome, old plastic body panels, mirrors, dim headlight, and cheap LED reflections matter more than brand or performance.

## Must Keep

- Used street motorcycle / retro scooter scale.
- Wet chrome, mirrors, worn plastic panels.
- Single warm headlight or slightly aged lamp.
- No visible brand logo.
- Grounded local practicality: something parked near repair shops, night markets, field roads.
- Works at low speed; no racing posture.

## Avoid

- Luxury superbike, sci-fi bike, racing fairings.
- Brand logos, decals, readable plate text.
- Clean showroom polish.
- High-speed action-chase framing.
