# Color and Lighting Lock V1

Status: active visual lock.

## Palette

- Red LED: longing, separation, karaoke/market edge.
- Green fluorescent: county-town cheap LED, wet alley, plastic tarp.
- Amber bulbs: game booth, beer stall, warmth, human memory.
- Cyan-blue shadows: wet pavement, pre-dawn air, field-road ending.
- White light boxes: only abstract overexposed blocks, never readable signs.

## Progression

1. Night-market entry: red/green/amber all active, wet and dense.
2. Boy and girl first anchors: foreground obstruction and warm bulbs.
3. Alley and repair-shop: red/green reflections become sharper and more claustrophobic.
4. Wholesale market: color blocks get larger and emptier.
5. Field road: neon recedes; cyan-blue morning takes over.

## Exposure Rules

- Preserve deep shadows and wet highlight bloom.
- Let cheap lights overexpose locally, but do not blow out the entire frame.
- Use smoke, steam, plastic, mirror, and puddles as light carriers.
- Avoid HDR crispness, clean studio light, and equal clarity everywhere.
