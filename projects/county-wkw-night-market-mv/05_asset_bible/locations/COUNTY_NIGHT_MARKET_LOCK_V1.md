# County Night Market Lock V1

Status: active environment lock.

## World

Chinese商贸县城 summer night after rain. The place is ordinary, low-cost, and local, but photographed with romantic neon and wet reflections.

## Required Locations

- Night-market entrance after rain.
- Cheap LED awning and beer-stall edge.
- Game booth with bulbs and plastic prizes.
- Beer/barbecue stall with plastic stools and smoke.
- Narrow wet alley behind the market.
- Anonymous karaoke entrance and repair-shop corner.
- Empty wholesale-market gate before dawn.
- Field road outside town.

## Required Texture

- Plastic tarps, rain curtains, puddles, wet asphalt/concrete.
- E-bikes, used motorcycle, beer crates, plastic stools.
- Blurred bulbs, smoke, steam, oily repair-shop water.
- Anonymous light boxes and unreadable colored blocks.
- Ordinary county-town scale: low buildings, shutters, alleys, open market gates.

## Avoid

- International skyline, megacity density, luxury shopping mall.
- Clean future lab or premium cyberpunk architecture.
- Readable signage, real brands, fake generated text.
- Overly empty abstract neon without county-town objects.
