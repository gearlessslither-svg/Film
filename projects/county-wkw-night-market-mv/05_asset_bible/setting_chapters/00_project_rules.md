# CH00 Project Rules - 县城王家卫

## Classification

This is a music MV project: weak narrative, strong emotional line, strong visual rhythm. It does not require the full story/dialogue approval gate used by narrative projects, but it does require a clear emotional arc, visual identity, and continuity locks before large-batch production.

## Core Promise

The film should feel like Chinese county-town romance made cinematic: night-market tarps, plastic stools, beer crates, e-bikes, game-booth bulbs, repair shops, anonymous karaoke entrances, wholesale-market gates, wet alleys, and field roads, transformed by rain, cheap LED, reflections, smoke, and longing.

## Hard Rules

- Use "县城王家卫" only as human shorthand. Production prompts must translate it into explicit visual language.
- Every production prompt deliverable must be bilingual Chinese + English.
- Every image-to-video or AIGC video prompt must specify sound effects / ambience only; no music, no BGM, no soundtrack.
- No readable shop signs, real brands, logos, watermarks, subtitles, or broken text.
- No glossy international metropolis skyline, cyberpunk megacity, clean futuristic laboratory, luxury superbike, or sci-fi vehicle design.
- Keep the girl romantic and grounded, not sexualized.
- Keep the boy ordinary and quiet, not a rebel caricature or fashion model.
- Keep the motorcycle used, wet, ordinary, and local.
- For large batches, generate in small groups and update handoff after each batch.
