# CH01 Character Locks - Boy and Girl

## Boy Lock Summary

Reference mood frames:

- `LD002_boy_motorcycle_led_awning.png`
- `LD005_red_green_alley_ride.png`
- `LD006_ktv_repair_shop_reflection.png`
- `LD008_field_road_before_dawn.png`

Must keep:

- Early 20s Chinese county-town motorcycle youth.
- Quiet, restrained, slightly tired face; not rebellious exaggeration.
- Slightly wet dark hair, natural and unstyled.
- Worn dark jacket or dark windbreaker, damp fabric, ordinary local clothing.
- Slim-to-average build; no heroic bodybuilder silhouette.
- Emotional function: he carries the ride, the hesitation, and the unsaid confession.

Avoid:

- Celebrity likeness.
- Fashion editorial styling.
- Luxury biker image, racing suit, branded helmet, leather-gang stereotype.
- Too-clean idol face or anime prettiness.

## Girl Lock Summary

Reference mood frames:

- `LD003_girl_game_booth_bulbs.png`
- `LD004_beer_stall_smoke_crossing.png`
- `LD006_ktv_repair_shop_reflection.png`
- `LD007_wholesale_market_gate.png`
- `LD008_field_road_before_dawn.png`

Must keep:

- Early 20s Chinese county-town young woman.
- Grounded summer-night styling: simple light jacket, plain shirt, skirt/dress/trousers that feel local and ordinary.
- Reflective eyes, mysterious but real.
- She often appears through bulbs, smoke, rain curtains, glass, mirror, or distance.
- Emotional function: she is not a fantasy object; she is the person who turns an ordinary county night into memory.

Avoid:

- Sexualized pose, pin-up framing, or glamour-club styling.
- Celebrity likeness.
- Overly polished fashion campaign styling.
- High-school/minor ambiguity; she must read as an adult in her early 20s.

## Two-Character Rule

They should rarely face each other in a clean romantic pose. The strongest grammar is almost-meeting:

- separated by smoke, plastic rain curtain, glass, mirror, street width, or wet ground;
- one turning while the other is partly hidden;
- both held in the same reflection but not the same physical space;
- distance increasing toward the field-road ending.
