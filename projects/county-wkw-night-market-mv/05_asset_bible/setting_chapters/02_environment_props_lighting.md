# CH02 Environment, Props, and Lighting Locks

## Environment Lock

The world is a Chinese商贸县城 summer night, not a futuristic city. It should feel like the edge of places around Hengdian/Yiwu-style trading towns: night markets, small entertainment streets, repair corners, wholesale gates, wet alleys, and fields just outside town.

Required object families:

- Plastic tarps and transparent rain curtains.
- Blue/red plastic stools.
- Green or anonymous beer crates with no readable brands.
- E-bikes and used ordinary motorcycles.
- Game-booth bulbs, plastic toys, ring-toss props, balloons.
- Barbecue smoke, steam, wet pavement, puddles.
- Half-open rolling shutters, repair-shop tools, tires, oil-stained rainwater.
- Anonymous karaoke light boxes, no readable `KTV` or shop names.
- Wholesale-market gate and closed rolling shutters.
- Field road, paddies/cornfields, low streetlights, distant county-town neon.

## Lighting Lock

Core palette:

- Red LED / neon spill.
- Green fluorescent tubes.
- Amber bulbs and streetlights.
- Cyan-blue wet shadows and pre-dawn air.
- White overexposed light boxes only as abstract blocks.

Rules:

- Neon must come from cheap local sources, not premium sci-fi architecture.
- Wet ground and plastic surfaces should carry the color.
- Smoke and steam should soften the image, not become magical VFX.
- The ending should let red/green/amber fade into cyan-blue morning.

## Camera Lock

Preferred grammar:

- 21:9 cinematic frames.
- Long lens / telephoto compression.
- Shallow depth of field.
- Off-center framing.
- Foreground obstruction with rain curtains, bulbs, shoulders, chair backs, mirrors, windows.
- Slow-shutter drag for movement, but keep characters readable.

Avoid:

- Clean symmetric hero-poster framing for every shot.
- Over-wide cyberpunk skyline views.
- Fast-action chase language.
- Sharp HDR clarity across every object.
