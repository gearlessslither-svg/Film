# Style Bible

## Human Shorthand

“县城王家卫”：县城夜市、男孩遇到女孩、机车、雨夜、暧昧、复古未来感。

## Prompt-Safe Visual Translation

Use explicit visual language instead of relying only on a named director:

- 1990s Hong Kong romantic neon cinema mood.
- Saturated red, green, amber, and cyan fluorescent spill.
- Rain-wet pavement, puddle reflections, smoke and steam.
- Slow-shutter motion blur, step-print rhythm, handheld close-ups.
- Off-center framing, reflections through glass, plastic curtains, mirrors.
- Telephoto compression, shallow depth of field, foreground obstruction.
- County-town Chinese texture: beer stalls, game booths, KTV entrances, repair shops, wholesale-market streets, plastic stools, e-bikes, cheap tarps, small alleys, fields outside town.

## Visual Red Lines

- No glossy international metropolis skyline.
- No luxury sci-fi vehicle design.
- No clean futuristic laboratory look.
- No readable shop text or brand logos.
- No exact celebrity likeness.
- Do not make the girl sexualized; keep it romantic and grounded.

## First Lookdev Batch Suggestion

1. Night-market entrance after rain.
2. Boy on motorcycle under cheap LED awning.
3. Girl at game booth through hanging bulbs.
4. Beer stall with plastic stools and smoke.
5. Alley ride with red-green reflections.
6. KTV entrance / repair shop corner.
7. Empty wholesale-market street.
8. Country road and field outside town before dawn.

## Prompt Delivery Rule

Every production prompt must include:

- 中文提示词
- English prompt
- Negative / avoid list
- For video: Chinese video prompt + English video prompt + sound effects only, no music/BGM.
