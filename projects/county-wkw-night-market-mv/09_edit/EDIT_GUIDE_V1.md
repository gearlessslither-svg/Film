## Recommended Cut

Target length: 60-90 seconds. First review cut: about 75 seconds.

The edit should feel like a music MV with weak narrative and strong visual memory. Do not over-explain the relationship. The power is in repeated almost-meetings, reflections, distance, and the ride out of the night market.

## Structure

| Section | Time | Frames | Edit Feeling |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Intro | 0-10s | KF001-KF002 | Enter the wet night-market dream; slow push, low engine, first mood lock. |
| First hook | 10-20s | KF003-KF004 | Girl appears; first almost-meeting through bulbs, smoke, and crowd. |
| Motion lift | 20-37s | KF005-KF007 | Motorcycle starts, alley ride, repair-shop reflection. |
| Breath | 37-43s | KF008 | Girl disappears behind rain curtain; let the frame breathe. |
| Late-night drop | 43-55s | KF009-KF010 | Empty wholesale market and tactile memory details. |
| Ride-out | 55-67s | KF011-KF012 | Neon recedes; field road and dawn begin. |
| Ending | 67-75s | KF013-KF014 | Girl goes distant; boy stops; no subtitle card. |

## Rhythm Notes

- Keep cuts slower than a trailer. This is not an action montage.
- Use KF010 as a rhythmic insert between wider emotional shots.
- Repeat wet reflections and plastic-rain-curtain occlusion as visual glue.
- Avoid overusing hard transitions. Prefer dissolves, motion blur, match cuts on reflected light, and slight speed ramps.
- If using CapCut/Jianying effects, use them as glue, not decoration. QA any interpolation on frames containing signs, light boxes, or fine high-contrast details.

## Audio Notes

- AIGC video clips should contain only ambience/sound effects from the prompt package.
- Add final music separately in editing.
- Keep usable sync sounds under the song if they help texture: engine idle, wet tires, rain tarp drops, neon buzz, barbecue sizzle, distant stall voices, field insects.
- Do not let any generated clip bring in its own music, BGM, soundtrack, singing, or score.

## Review Checklist

- Does every shot still feel like Chinese county-town night, not glossy metropolis cyberpunk?
- Do boy, girl, and motorcycle remain close enough to the V1 locks?
- Are all signs, light boxes, crates, and plates unreadable?
- Is the girl adult, grounded, and non-sexualized?
- Does the ending avoid explanatory text and melodrama?
- Does the whole piece feel like one summer night drifting from cheap neon to dawn?
