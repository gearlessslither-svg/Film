# Story Spine

## Form

Music MV, 60-90 seconds recommended for first cut.

## Emotional Arc

1. 男孩骑机车进入县城夜市，雨刚停，地面全是霓虹倒影。
2. 他在游戏摊或啤酒摊旁第一次看见女孩，隔着灯泡、烟、塑料雨布和人群。
3. 两个人没有明确对白，只靠目光、错身、跟拍和反复出现的机车声建立暧昧。
4. 他们穿过小巷、KTV 门口、修车铺、空旷批发市场门头。
5. 夜市尽头变成县城外的小路和田野，廉价霓虹被远处路灯和虫鸣替代。
6. 清晨前，女孩消失在路口或公交站，男孩停在田野边，像一场没发生完的夏夜梦。

## Character Anchors

- Boy: early 20s, county-town motorcycle youth, plain dark jacket, slightly wet hair, quiet face, not rebellious caricature.
- Girl: early 20s, casual summer county-town styling, light jacket or simple dress, reflective eyes, mysterious but grounded.
- Motorcycle: used street motorcycle or retro scooter, not luxury superbike, with wet chrome and cheap LED reflection.

## No-Dialogue Rule

First version should be almost no dialogue. If text is needed, use voiceover subtitles in editing, not generated in-image text.
