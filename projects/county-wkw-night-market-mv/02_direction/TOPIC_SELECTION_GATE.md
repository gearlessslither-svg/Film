# Topic Selection Gate

## Project Type

Music MV. Proceed after narrowing. The user has approved opening this project.

## Traffic / Audience Potential

Strong. “县城夜市 + 青春暧昧 + 低配赛博朋克 + 复古港片情绪” has broad recognition for Chinese audiences:

- County-town nightlife feels familiar and underused compared with generic futuristic city cyberpunk.
- Boy-meets-girl MV structure is immediately readable without heavy exposition.
- Night-market objects create comment-section memory triggers: beer stalls, game booths, plastic stools, electric scooters, KTV, rain, alleys, country roads.

## Aesthetic / Production Value

Strong. AIGC advantage is turning ordinary low-cost environments into cinematic romantic memory:

- Wet neon reflections and smoke can make cheap county materials beautiful.
- Slow-shutter blur and telephoto compression hide model flaws while increasing mood.
- A limited two-character anchor can keep continuity manageable.

## Likely Failure Points

- If it becomes generic cyberpunk, it loses the county-town soul.
- If it leans only on a named director reference, prompts become vague and inconsistent.
- If character locks are weak, boy/girl continuity will drift.
- Readable Chinese shop signs may become broken text; use illegible signage and light boxes.
- Too many crowds may reduce control; use silhouettes, backs, partial faces, reflections.

## Decision

Proceed, but narrow the first lookdev batch to 6-10 key mood frames before building the full MV.

## Required Stop-Loss Checks Before Generation

- Does each frame contain at least one unmistakable county-town object?
- Does it feel romantic and retro-future, not glossy metropolis sci-fi?
- Are the boy, girl, motorcycle, and night-market environment stable enough to become locks?
- Are prompts bilingual and video prompts audio-limited to sound effects / ambience only?
