## Global Avoid

中文：不要可读中文/英文/数字/店招/品牌/车牌/水印/字幕；不要名人脸；不要动漫脸；不要豪车、科幻摩托、赛博大都会、干净未来实验室；女孩不要性感化；人物必须是成年人二十出头。

English: No readable Chinese, English letters, numbers, signage, brands, license plates, watermarks, or subtitles; no celebrity likeness; no anime face; no luxury vehicle, sci-fi motorcycle, cyberpunk megacity, or clean futuristic laboratory; do not sexualize the girl; both characters must read as adults in their early 20s.
