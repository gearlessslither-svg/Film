# Arcade Cabinet Whitebox Handoff / 街机实体白模

Whitebox guidance / 白模读取说明
- Use this whitebox as the fixed two-player arcade cabinet prop lock: one shared CRT screen, left/right control stations, wide control deck, front coin slot, and two low stools.
- Preserve A Lei and the yellow-haired punk as side-by-side players facing the same screen; do not turn them face-to-face or toward camera in duel shots.
- Preserve screen/control-panel/stool height relationships for rear-head shots and game-screen inserts.
- Do not copy white material, primitive geometry, or clean test-render look; convert it into gritty 1990s Chinese arcade materials.
- Negative constraint: no modern LCD, no esports setup, no readable logos, no cyberpunk arcade.
