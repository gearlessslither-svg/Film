## English model prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 9:16 portrait three-environment lookdev board, no text. Image 1 is the sole DFT style authority. Image 2 is the approved Abai sheet and defines project brightness, palette translation and anthropomorphic scale only.

[NARRATIVE_TIME] Three separate location studies, not one continuous shot: FACTORY_GRAY_DAWN, LOCKER_ROOM_AFTER_SHIFT, CENTRAL_SQUARE_GOLDEN_EVENING.

[STYLE_FINGERPRINT] Old-paper egg tempera and matte gouache, restrained handmade dark linework, broad geometric architecture, flattened shallow perspective, slightly distorted vertical scale, deep teal-blue, ochre, faded brick red, aged mustard and cream. Quiet northwestern industrial fairy tale, serious and gently absurd, one stop brighter and warmer than DFT, controlled low-noise texture.

[SUBJECT_AND_SPACE] Divide the vertical sheet into three large separated environment vignettes with no borders or labels. Top: realistic human-scale northwestern red-brick factory exterior at gray dawn, tall chimney, old clock-in room, repeated windows, broad empty concrete yard, stopped conveyor structure, gray-blue haze; only four tiny generic animal-worker silhouettes in identical navy coveralls for scale, no named characters. Middle: high-angle public industrial locker room with high windows, long wooden benches, rows of deep-teal metal lockers, worn concrete floor, warm-gray practical lights, identical navy work clothes folded in repeated orderly stacks; corridors wide enough for an elephant, no people. Bottom: small-town central square enclosed by red-brick dormitories and an old community hall, low wooden platform, modest string lights, long tables and an open dance floor, distant factory chimney visible on the same axis; blue-gray dusk shifting into ochre-gold and faded red, no crowd yet.

[CAMERA_AND_COMPOSITION] Each vignette has clear foreground, midground and background, functional human industrial scale, stable frontal or slight high-angle views, large shapes before detail, generous quiet negative space. No toy town, no fantasy architecture, no text, signs, labels, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, or decorative clutter.

[LIGHTING] Top cool diffuse dawn with one weak mustard practical; middle soft warm-gray overhead practicals and blue window fill; bottom motivated ochre string lights against blue-gray dusk. Matte shadows, no magical glow.

[INTENTIONAL_REALITY_EXCEPTIONS] Only anthropomorphic worker silhouettes. Architecture, circulation, load, scale and lighting remain physically plausible.

[NEGATIVE] No Abai close-up, no repeated named character, no human figures, no human hands, no animal toy city, no candy colors, no ruined apocalypse, no heavy fog, no horror, supernatural light, photorealism, 3D, anime, Disney style, random grime, object clutter, pseudo-text, watermark, film grain, random speckle, muddy microtexture, fake background symbols.
