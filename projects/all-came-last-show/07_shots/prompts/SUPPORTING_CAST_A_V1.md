# 配角动物人设板 A / Supporting Cast A

## 中文审阅摘要

9:16 无文字双状态人设板：年长绵羊、中年大象、瘦高驴、老狗；每个角色并列工厂统一工装与演出私人服装。DFT 参考只锁媒介，批准的阿白板锁项目内比例、亮度和服装翻译。禁止人手、额外肢体、可爱吉祥物、破损乐器与伪文字。

## English model prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 9:16 portrait supporting-cast dual-state character sheet, no text. Image 1 is the sole DFT style authority. Image 2 is the approved Abai sheet and defines this project's anthropomorphic proportion system, brightness, matte costume treatment, and level of simplification; do not copy Abai's cat identity or markings.

[NARRATIVE_TIME] Non-narrative design sheet comparing FACTORY_LAST_SHIFT and AFTER_CHANGING_BEFORE_FAREWELL for four separate supporting characters.

[STYLE_FINGERPRINT] Old-paper egg tempera and matte gouache, handmade restrained dark linework, broad flat geometric shapes, shallow space, slightly awkward elongated fairy-tale proportions, deep teal, ochre, aged mustard, cream, faded brick red. Quiet and gently absurd, one stop brighter and warmer than the DFT reference. Group fur, wool and skin into clean painted masses; low noise, no photoreal hair.

[SUBJECT] Four clearly separated character columns, each with two smaller full-body states that preserve one identity: (1) elderly sheep, gray-white wool, long narrow face, small natural curved horns; deep-navy factory coveralls, then aged-mustard shirt and deep-teal shoulder wrap, holding one correctly scaled complete violin and bow with a plausible simplified hoof grip. (2) middle-aged elephant, largest and heaviest figure, blue-gray skin; oversized deep-navy coveralls, then a faded brick-red formal jacket visibly too small but still structurally wearable, calmly trying one button, no acrobatics. (3) tall lean donkey, gray-brown coat, long ears, slight stoop; deep-navy coveralls, then cream shirt and worn ochre suspenders, holding one structurally complete old cornet. (4) older dog, coarse brown-black fur, drooping ears, broad shoulders; deep-navy coveralls, then oxidized-green waistcoat and faded-red scarf, holding one small hand drum.

[CAMERA_AND_COMPOSITION] Calm orthographic model-sheet layout, eye level, full bodies readable, two states per character close enough to compare but never overlapping. One consistent scale hierarchy, elephant largest. Generous warm-paper negative space. No text, labels, letters, numbers, logo, border, or decorative clutter.

[LIGHTING] Soft diffuse ochre key, weak gray-blue fill, matte pigments, no plastic shine.

[INTENTIONAL_REALITY_EXCEPTIONS] Upright anthropomorphic posture, clothing, instruments. All limb counts, weight, feet, grips, instrument structure and garment construction otherwise obey ordinary reality.

[NEGATIVE] No Abai duplicate, no human hands or exposed human fingers, no extra/missing limbs, duplicate heads or tails, quadruped pose, mascot cuteness, giant cartoon eyes, comedy faces, circus elephant, broken instruments, missing strings/valves, mixed costumes, photorealism, 3D, anime, Disney style, horror, random speckle, muddy microtexture, pseudo-text, watermark.
