# 阿白双状态人设板 V1 / Abai Dual-State Character Sheet V1

## 中文审阅版

用途：`illustration-story`，项目首轮角色 lookdev 候选；9:16 纵向无文字人设板。

[NARRATIVE_TIME] 非叙事角色设计板；并列展示 `FACTORY_LAST_SHIFT` 与 `AFTER_CHANGING_BEFORE_FAREWELL`，不是同一时刻的多人场景。

[CHARACTER_STATE_LOCK]
- 左侧主视图：`ABAI_FACTORY_UNIFORM`。年轻拟人混种白猫，温暖奶白短毛，旧琥珀眼，额前正中仅一撮上翘炭黑毛；深蓝旧棉工装与同色工帽完整遮住左肋黑纹；空手，奶白尾巴可见。
- 右侧主视图：`ABAI_PERFORMANCE_DRESS`。同一只阿白、同一脸型与比例；略宽奶白衬衫、旧赭红背心、深青短外套；身体仅一条窄而不规则的炭黑纹从左肋连续绕到背部；持尺寸合适、结构完整、仅普通磨损的旧木小提琴与琴弓；奶白尾巴末端无黑色。

[STATE_TRANSITION_RULE] 人设板只做状态并列，不在任何单独视图中变装或混穿。黑纹在工厂状态完全被遮住，在演出状态可见且始终只有同一条。

[STYLE_FINGERPRINT] 严格继承所附 DFT 参考图的视觉语言而不复制其人物、道具或故事：旧纸上的蛋彩／水粉，哑光颜料，细密而朴拙的深色手工描线，平面化透视，几何化大形，稍显笨拙的修长比例，深青蓝、赭金、旧芥末、奶白与少量褪色砖红；安静、克制、略荒诞的 1990 年代中国美术片气质。整体比参考亮一档、温暖一档，不恐怖、不阴森。保留真实纸张和颜料触感，但纹理受控、低噪、无随机颗粒。

[SUBJECT_AND_ACTION] 一张完整角色设计板。上方约三分之二放两个较大的完整全身主视图：左为工厂状态正面，右为演出状态略转向镜头的正面，让左肋唯一黑纹可读，并自然持琴。下方约三分之一安排四个较小、留白分隔的研究：一个侧面全身、一个背面全身，以及两个头像表情——安静疲惫、克制地微微放松。所有视图保持同一脸型、体型、毛色、耳形、额前黑毛、尾长和服装结构；表情不卖萌。

[CAMERA_AND_COMPOSITION] 9:16 纵向旧纸角色板，正面平视，稳定、平面、近似舞台陈列；大视图先读，小视图次读；轮廓互不重叠，四周与视图之间有宽松负空间。无网格、无文字、无编号、无标注、无装饰性道具堆砌。

[LIGHTING] 柔和漫射棚光，左上方温暖赭金主光，极弱灰蓝环境补光，轻微深青轮廓分离；哑光表面，无塑料高光，无戏剧性黑影。背景为均匀温暖旧纸色，边缘轻微暗化但不脏。

[SPACE_AND_CONTINUITY] 每个视图都是独立的设计研究，不是同一叙事空间。工装遮纹；演出服露出同一条左肋绕背黑纹。小提琴大小、结构、木色一致。所有足爪稳稳落地，持琴接触可信。

[INTENTIONAL_REALITY_EXCEPTIONS] 仅允许拟人动物直立、穿衣与持琴；人设板中同一角色的多视图并列是设计惯例，不代表复制角色。除此之外遵守正常解剖、重力、抓握和衣物结构。

[NEGATIVE] 禁止参考图里的具体人物、月亮、鸟、舟、火焰与叙事事件；禁止写实绘本、照片写实、3D、日漫、迪士尼式可爱、吉祥物、恐怖黑暗童话、红眼、尖牙、巨大卡通眼。禁止人手、裸露五指、额外或缺失肢体、重复尾巴、四足姿态、错误抓握、漂浮足爪。禁止额外黑斑、第二条身体黑纹、黑尾尖、黑耳、黑眼圈；禁止工装与演出服混穿；禁止破琴、缺弦、夸张修补。禁止伪文字、字母、数字、logo、水印、边框。画面必须清晰高分辨率、低噪、细节密度受控、轮廓自然清楚、平面区域渐变平滑；无胶片颗粒、随机斑点、泥状微纹理、过锐光晕、假像素细节、JPEG 压缩痕迹和嘈杂背景。

## English model-facing version

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: project character lookdev candidate, vertical dual-state model sheet, no text
Input images: Image 1 is the sole hard style reference; inherit only its visual language, never its characters, props, moon, birds, boats, fire, or story events.

[NARRATIVE_TIME] A non-narrative character design sheet comparing FACTORY_LAST_SHIFT and AFTER_CHANGING_BEFORE_FAREWELL; the repeated views are separate design studies, not multiple characters sharing one story moment.

[CHARACTER_STATE_LOCK]
- Left hero view, ABAI_FACTORY_UNIFORM: the same young anthropomorphic mixed-breed white cat, slim but healthy, warm cream short fur, normal-sized aged-amber eyes, exactly one small upward charcoal-black forelock centered on the forehead. Worn deep-navy cotton factory coveralls and matching cap fully hide the left-rib body stripe. Empty paws. One complete cream tail.
- Right hero view, ABAI_PERFORMANCE_DRESS: exactly the same Abai identity and proportions. Slightly oversized cream shirt, worn ochre-red waistcoat, short deep-teal jacket. Exactly one narrow irregular charcoal-black body stripe crosses the left ribs and continues around onto the back; no other body markings. Naturally holds one correctly scaled, structurally complete, ordinarily worn wooden violin and bow. The tail remains fully cream, including its tip.

[STATE_TRANSITION_RULE] The sheet compares two locked states only. Never mix costumes within one view. The body stripe is fully covered in factory state and visible only in performance state; it is always one continuous stripe, never duplicated.

[STYLE_FINGERPRINT] Faithfully inherit the supplied DFT reference image's old-paper egg-tempera and matte gouache visual language: restrained handmade dark linework, broad geometric readable shapes, flattened perspective, slightly awkward elongated proportions, deep teal-blue, ochre gold, aged mustard, cream, and a little faded brick red. Quiet, solemn, gently absurd fairy-tale staging with the feeling of a 1990s Chinese art-film illustration. Make it one stop brighter and warmer than the reference, without horror or ominous darkness. Tactile paper and matte pigment, but controlled texture and low noise. Do not imitate or reproduce any reference subject or scene.

[SUBJECT_AND_ACTION] One complete vertical character design sheet. Upper two-thirds: two large separated full-body hero studies, factory-state front view on the left and performance-state near-front view on the right, torso turned just enough for the single left-rib stripe to read while the violin is held naturally. Lower third: four smaller, clearly separated studies with generous blank space—one full-body side view, one full-body back view, and two head-expression studies: quietly tired; restrained slight release. Every study preserves exactly the same face, head shape, ears, body proportions, fur color, forelock, tail length, costume construction, and stripe continuity. Expressions are never cute or exaggerated.

[CAMERA_AND_COMPOSITION] 9:16 portrait old-paper model sheet, eye-level orthographic presentation, calm flat theatrical arrangement. Large hero views read first and the four small studies read second. No overlapping silhouettes. Generous negative space around all studies. No grid, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no decorative object parade.

[LIGHTING] Soft diffuse studio illumination; warm ochre key from upper left, very weak gray-blue fill, subtle deep-teal separation. Matte surfaces, no plastic shine, no dramatic black shadows. Even warm aged-paper background with only a very gentle clean edge vignette.

[SPACE_AND_CONTINUITY] Each view is an independent design study. Factory clothing hides the stripe. Performance clothing exposes the same single left-rib stripe wrapping to the back. Violin scale, construction, wood color, and ordinary wear stay identical. Feet are grounded and violin contact is physically plausible.

[INTENTIONAL_REALITY_EXCEPTIONS] Only anthropomorphic upright posture, clothing, and violin playing are intentional. Repeated views on the sheet are standard design-sheet convention, not character duplication. All other anatomy, gravity, garment construction, grip, and contact obey ordinary reality.

[NEGATIVE] No specific characters, moon, birds, boats, flame, or story events from the reference. No realistic storybook painting, photorealism, 3D, anime, Disney-like cuteness, mascot design, horror, red eyes, fangs, or huge cartoon eyes. No human hands, exposed human fingers, extra or missing limbs, duplicated tails, quadruped pose, broken grip, or floating feet. No extra black marks, second body stripe, black tail tip, black ears, or eye patches. No mixed factory/performance clothing. No broken violin, missing strings, or symbolic repairs. No pseudo-text, typography, letters, numbers, logo, watermark, border, or frame. Clean high-resolution image with low noise, controlled detail density, crisp readable silhouettes, smooth gradients in flat areas, natural edge transitions, no film grain, no random speckle, no muddy micro-texture, no over-sharpened halos, no fake pixel-level detail, no JPEG artifacts, and no noisy background.
