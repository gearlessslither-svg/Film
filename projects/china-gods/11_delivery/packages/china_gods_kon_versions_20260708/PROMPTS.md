# Kon-Language Generation Prompts

Global style language for every image:

21:9 cinematic frame, adult psychological Japanese animation, urban realism colliding with dream logic, hand-painted cel-animation look, elegant non-cute character design, precise cinematic blocking, layered reflections, screens, glass, surveillance angles, crowd pressure, identity slippage, beautiful but unsettling. Keep the Chinese mythological subject and modern social setting. No readable text, no brands, no subtitles, no watermark. Avoid generic anime, chibi, game fantasy, plastic 3D, cheap meme poster, and overdesigned armor.
