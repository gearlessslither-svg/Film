# Brutal Ink Omen Full Image Prompts V2

Project: `dragon-house-black-green-ink-wash-title-21x9`
Active style: `Brutal Ink Dragon Omen`
Sole visual reference: `04_lookdev/style_references/brutal_ink_omen_v1/director_reference_only_style.png`
Aspect: 21:9

Do not use shadow-puppet, cut-paper, leather-puppet, ornate-card, or soft scenic ink experiments. The only style to learn is the director reference: brutal splashed black ink dragon, old paper, one cold figure, sparse mineral green and cinnabar, severe negative space.

## Global Prompt

Create one 21:9 ultrawide cinematic keyframe in brutal Chinese splashed-ink xieyi. Old rice-paper ground, one overwhelming black ink dragon or power omen made from violent dry-brush sweeps, ink splatter, flying-white brush gaps, smoky gray wash, sparse mineral green stain, a few cinnabar seal-like red blocks with no readable characters, faint antique-gold cracks only as subtle texture. Severe minimalist composition, high clarity, crisp silhouette, controlled paper grain, low noise. The frame must read as one lethal ink gesture plus one isolated figure or symbol.

## Global Negative

No shadow puppet, no cut-paper theatre, no leather puppet, no puppet rods, no hinge joints, no ornate stage frame, no crowded palace scenery, no battlefield crowd, no soft decorative watercolor, no decorative fantasy poster, no readable text, no logo, no watermark, no actor likeness, no exact official costume, no photoreal dragon flesh, no anime, no western fantasy concept-art realism.

## Opening 10s Keyframes

### Opening Start Frame

Use the global prompt. A blank old rice-paper field fills the 21:9 frame. From the left edge, a single black ink dragon head begins to form out of a violent dry-brush sweep, still half abstract. A tiny cold crown-like figure/symbol stands low center as a black vertical mark. Sparse mineral green stain bleeds at far right, two cinnabar blocks sit far apart. Composition is almost empty, tense, before the omen fully arrives.

### Opening End Frame

Use the global prompt. The black ink dragon has completed a huge circular strike across the entire frame, head pressing down above a lone black throne/crown silhouette in lower center. The dragon is still brush-ink, not flesh. Paper negative space remains large and pale. Sparse mineral green stain and cinnabar blocks balance the right side. The image feels like a death sentence closing.

## 14 Story Keyframes

### 01 Bloodline Opens

Use the global prompt. A black dragon-stroke cuts open the paper from lower left to upper right like a torn bloodline. At the crack, two tiny crown silhouettes split apart, one black and one mineral-green stain. No map or landscape. The whole idea is one slash, one split, one inherited curse.

### 02 Disputed Queen

Use the global prompt. A cold full-body queen-like figure stands lower left, mask-like face visible but not realistic. Above her, a vast black dragon arcs as one ink storm, its head almost touching her crown. A pale empty crown shape floats in negative space on the right. The frame is severe, not court scenery.

### 03 Rogue Prince

Use the global prompt. A full-body rogue prince figure stands lower center-left, tall dark robe/armor silhouette, pale hair as vertical ink threads, mask-like face visible. A red-black serpentine dragon omen coils overhead as one violent sweep, with a narrow jaw and horn strokes. Sparse cinnabar at the lower right, restrained gold cracks on the robe.

### 04 Green Court

Use the global prompt. Replace the council scene with one isolated green-faction queen/mother symbol: a rigid dark figure lower center, face half hidden by black wash, standing beneath a descending dragon-shaped black political shadow. Mineral green stain spreads behind her like poisoned court air. No table, no seated crowd.

### 05 Coronation Omen

Use the global prompt. A crooked crown becomes the isolated subject: lower center, black and antique-gold, small but sharp. Above it, a huge black dragon head forms a circular halo of ink, like a broken coronation sun. Sparse cinnabar blocks mark the paper edges. No ruler crowd, no palace.

### 06 War Curse

Use the global prompt. A tiny one-eyed warrior/prince silhouette stands lower right, full body visible. A massive ancient dragon skull descends from upper left as a black ink storm, with one mineral-green eye stain. Keep strong negative space and a lethal scale difference.

### 07 Naval Power

Use the global prompt. A sea-lord symbol replaces scenic fleets: one black sea-serpent prow rises lower center as an angular vertical mark. A dragon-shaped ink wave curls above like a storm tide. Sparse mineral green wash only at the base, no busy ships, no horizon scene.

### 08 Young Heirs

Use the global prompt. Two small young heir silhouettes stand far apart inside torn white negative space, one left, one right. Above them, a single black dragon stroke breaks into two fading tails, as if inheritance is splitting. Use emptiness and menace, not landscape.

### 09 Dream Prophecy

Use the global prompt. A small still prophetic figure stands lower center, barely moving, surrounded by pale paper. Above, the dragon is a soft but huge black ink spiral with moth-like splatter fragments. Add one faint mineral-green stain, almost dreamlike but still severe.

### 10 Martyr Rupture

Use the global prompt. A broken red-gold sun disc is reduced to one cracked circular ink omen behind a small noble bust/figure at lower center. Black dragon wing strokes tear through the sun shape. Keep the frame ritual, empty, and fatal.

### 11 Political Cage

Use the global prompt. A tiny advisor figure sits or stands lower center inside a few black vertical brush bars. Above, huge black ink hands become a dragon-like shadow closing around him. Sparse unreadable cinnabar seal blocks on the paper, no documents, no readable marks.

### 12 Oath To War

Use the global prompt. One black blade stroke rises from the lower edge like a vertical execution mark. A commander silhouette stands behind it, full body readable but small. A dragon-shaped ink wave curls over the blade. No army rows, no battlefield crowd.

### 13 Dragon War Tableau

Use the global prompt. Two huge dragon strokes collide across the frame: dense black from left, mineral-green-black from right. In the collision, one tiny crown silhouette is nearly erased. Keep it abstract and violent; no armies, no wide battlefield scene.

### 14 Final Story Aftermath

Use the global prompt. A lone broken crown lies lower center on old paper. Above it, the black dragon stroke has become a fading circular stain, head barely visible, as if power has consumed itself. Sparse red blocks and mineral green residue remain. The ending is quiet, ruthless, and final.
