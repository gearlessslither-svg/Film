## Keyframes

01 Bloodline Opens: low view over a rice-paper genealogy scroll. One black ink river cracks open from foreground to horizon while mineral-green wash pulls away on the opposite side. Palace eaves and mountains barely form from diluted ink in the distance. Large negative space, clean silhouettes.

02 Disputed Queen: a huge cropped queen-mask silhouette at the far left as a single black brush mass. Behind translucent ink palace eaves, a vast dragon shape appears only as a gray wash. Mineral green mist creeps through the court. No face likeness, no costume detail.

03 Rogue Prince: diagonal palace corridor made of slashing dry-brush strokes. A cropped prince sleeve cuts like a blade at foreground right. A red cinnabar dragon stroke whips across the middle distance, half abstract calligraphy, half dragon shadow.

04 Green Court: long council table as a black horizontal wash, tiny seated silhouettes, green mist pooling under an empty throne shape in negative space. Candle points become tiny cinnabar dots. Quiet political pressure, not action.

05 Coronation Omen: enormous crooked crown arc painted in black and antique gold wash across the foreground. A small ruler silhouette rises below it. A broken sun-dragon halo opens behind as cinnabar and gold ink bloom.

06 War Curse: a vast ancient dragon skull suggested by one heavy black wash descends from upper left. A tiny warrior silhouette stands lower right in white negative space. One mineral-green eye glow as a small stain through paper.

07 Naval Power: sea-serpent prow formed from a thick black brush stroke in foreground. Paper fleets cross a pale ink-sea horizon, rigging as dry-brush lines, green wash under the waves. Wide and low.

08 Young Heirs: two small heir silhouettes stand on opposite sides of torn white negative space, like a broken bridge. Small black and green dragon wisps spiral above them. Sparse composition, emotional distance.

09 Dream Prophecy: moth-like ink flecks orbit a pale chamber. A translucent dragon wing emerges behind gauze-like diluted wash. The dreamer silhouette is small and still, surrounded by soft negative space.

10 Martyr Rupture: red-gold sun disc fractures across the frame with wet ink cracks. A small central bust silhouette is surrounded by dragon wing shards and smoke-like wash blooms. Tragic, ceremonial.

11 Political Cage: giant black brush hands, stamp blocks, and unreadable document strips close around a tiny advisor figure. Lattice cage made from ink strokes and negative space, with restrained cinnabar seal marks.

12 Oath To War: low foreground blade as one long black brush stroke. Commander silhouette raises an arm. Banners and army rows become horizontal ink masses advancing across a misty horizon.

13 Dragon War Tableau: black and green armies collide as layered ink stains. Two huge dragon strokes cross diagonally above the battlefield, one dense black, one mineral-green wash. Rice paper flares pale at the collision.

14 Final Story Aftermath: broken black and green crown strokes lie in the foreground. Snapped ink bloodline streams hang loose across torn paper. Empty throne recedes in mist. Fading dragon afterimages remain on ruined parchment, quiet and final.
