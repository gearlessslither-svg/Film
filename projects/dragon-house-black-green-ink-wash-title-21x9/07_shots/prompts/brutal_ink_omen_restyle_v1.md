## Global Image Prompt

Create one 21:9 ultrawide cinematic keyframe in brutal Chinese splashed-ink xieyi. Old rice-paper ground, one huge black ink dragon or power omen made from violent dry-brush sweeps, ink splatter, flying-white brush gaps, smoky gray wash, sparse mineral green stain, a few cinnabar seal-like red blocks with no readable characters, faint antique-gold cracks only as subtle texture. Severe minimalist composition, high clarity, crisp silhouette, controlled paper grain, low noise.

The image must feel clean, ruthless, imperial, and lethal. It should read as one overwhelming ink gesture plus one isolated figure or symbol, not a scene illustration.
