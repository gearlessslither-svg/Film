## Global Motion Rule

Animate ink, paper, breath, and omen pressure. Motion comes from black brush mass spreading, dry-brush fibers pulling, ink splatter blooming, old paper breathing, mineral-green stains bleeding, cinnabar blocks pulsing faintly, and the dragon stroke sliding like a curse across the paper. Keep subjects iconic and stable; do not turn the dragon into realistic flesh or smooth creature animation.

Hard audio rule for every AIGC image-to-video prompt in this file: sound effects and ambience only. No music, no BGM, no soundtrack. Suggested sound palette: dry brush scrape, paper fiber rustle, ink bloom, distant low dragon breath, seal-stamp thud, mineral stain hiss, and designed silence.

Global negative: no hard cuts inside a shot, no puppet mechanics, no rods, no hinges, no ornate frame, no busy environment, no readable text, no logo, no watermark, no photoreal dragon, no actor likeness, no rubbery morphing, no random camera shake.

## Opening 10s One-Take

Inputs after regeneration:

- `06_previs/blender/brutal_ink_opening_one_take_10s_v1/inputs/start_frame.png`
- `06_previs/blender/brutal_ink_opening_one_take_10s_v1/inputs/end_frame.png`

Prompt:

Create a 10-second 21:9 one-take brutal Chinese splashed-ink title omen. No cuts. Start with old pale rice paper and the first black dragon head forming from a left-edge dry-brush sweep. The camera glides slowly forward into the paper while the black dragon stroke expands and curls. A lone crown/throne silhouette remains low center, still and cold. Mineral green stain bleeds from the right, cinnabar blocks pulse once, then the dragon closes into a vast circular omen over the throne by the final frame.

Timing:

- 0.0-2.0s: paper breathes; first black dragon-head stroke scratches into existence.
- 2.0-4.0s: brush body sweeps across the top half; ink splatter blooms and dries.
- 4.0-6.5s: camera creeps inward; green stain bleeds opposite the black stroke; the lone throne/crown silhouette sharpens.
- 6.5-8.5s: dragon body completes a circular pressure around the subject; cinnabar marks pulse faintly.
- 8.5-10.0s: settle on the completed omen, huge black dragon over the isolated power symbol, no title text.

Blender note: the old proxy MP4 is not compatible as a visual reference. If a motion reference is required, build a new 2.5D/Blender brutal-ink reference based on the regenerated start/end frames.

## Story Motions

### 01 Bloodline Opens

The black dragon slash tears the paper diagonally. Two small crown marks separate as the tear widens. Camera pushes along the slash, then locks on the split.

### 02 Disputed Queen

The queen figure holds still. The overhead dragon stroke lowers a few pixels and darkens near her crown. Paper fibers and splatter drift, but the figure remains iconic.

### 03 Rogue Prince

The red-black dragon arc whips once in a delayed ink wave. The prince robe edge barely lifts as if pulled by pressure. Camera makes a slight diagonal push toward the face.

### 04 Green Court

Mineral green stain spreads behind the rigid figure. The political dragon shadow descends and partially cages the negative space around her. Keep the figure still.

### 05 Coronation Omen

The crooked crown stays low and sharp. The dragon halo forms from rotating black brush arcs above it. Cinnabar blocks pulse once, then dim.

### 06 War Curse

The tiny warrior remains still. The ancient dragon skull descends from upper left by layered ink-darkening, not creature animation. One green eye stain brightens briefly.

### 07 Naval Power

The sea-serpent prow rises like a black vertical mark. The ink-wave dragon curls over it and dries into a dark tide. No ships or crowd movement.

### 08 Young Heirs

The two heir silhouettes do not move. The single dragon stroke above splits into two fading tails, then leaves torn white negative space between them.

### 09 Dream Prophecy

Moth-like splatter fragments orbit slowly around the prophetic figure. The dragon spiral breathes in gray wash, then returns to black stillness.

### 10 Martyr Rupture

The red-gold sun disc cracks once. Black dragon wing strokes tear through the circle and then freeze. The central noble figure remains ceremonial and still.

### 11 Political Cage

Vertical ink bars close around the tiny advisor. Huge hand/dragon shadows darken overhead. Cinnabar seal blocks flash without readable text.

### 12 Oath To War

The blade stroke rises from the lower edge in one severe motion. A dragon-shaped ink wave curls above and settles into black. The commander silhouette remains locked.

### 13 Dragon War Tableau

Two dragon strokes collide from left and right. The center flares pale as paper negative space, then the tiny crown silhouette is nearly swallowed by ink.

### 14 Final Story Aftermath

The broken crown lies still. The dragon stroke fades into a circular stain above it. Mineral green residue and cinnabar marks dry out. End quiet and final.
