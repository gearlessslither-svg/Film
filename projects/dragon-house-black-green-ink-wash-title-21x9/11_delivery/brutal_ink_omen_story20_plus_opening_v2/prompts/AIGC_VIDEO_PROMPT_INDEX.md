## Opening 9s One-Take

Images:
- Start: `images/opening_9s/opening_9s_v2_start_traveling_power_map_21x9.png`
- End: `images/opening_9s/opening_9s_v2_end_aerial_power_map_dragon_eclipse_21x9.png`

Prompt:
Create a 9-second 21:9 one-take image-to-video shot using the start and end frames. The feeling is inspired by the spatial journey of a fantasy title-sequence power map, but transformed into brutal Chinese ink. The camera must travel through the map, not simply watch buildings rise in place. Start at an extreme low macro angle beside a huge throne-gear and black bloodline canal. Glide forward along the ink canal, pass rising castle nodes, bridge ribs, crown gears, sea channels, and dragon-bone arcs, then climb into a high oblique aerial view where the whole dynasty map spreads out under a vast black dragon eclipse. Do not copy any official title design or readable map.

景别 / Shot size: starts as low macro travelling shot over a giant foreground mechanism; ends as expansive high oblique aerial map view.

机位 / Camera position: start with the camera skimming just above old rice paper beside a cropped throne-gear/crown-wheel. End high above the activated map, looking across islands, sea channels, raised castle mechanisms, and the dragon eclipse.

相机移动 / Camera movement: one continuous forward travelling shot plus gradual crane-up and slight clockwise orbit. No cuts. The camera moves along the black ink bloodline canal, passes multiple map nodes, then rises into a broad aerial reveal by the final second.

光影 / Lighting: pale rice-paper base light at the start; black ink channels darken as the camera passes; mineral-green poison glows in selected waterways and grooves; cinnabar nodes flare at distant corners; antique-gold cracks flicker along raised castle and gear edges.

动作 / Action: the bloodline canal fills ahead of the moving camera. Foreground throne gear rotates and falls behind camera. Castle blocks and bridge ribs rise as the camera passes them. Sea channels open as flying-white paper gaps. Dragon-bone arcs unfold along the route. In the final aerial reveal, a huge dry-brush dragon omen sweeps diagonally over the full map like an ink eclipse.

人物/表情 / Character and expression: no human faces. Power is shown through mechanisms, castles, crown gears, and dragon shadow.

情绪 / Emotion: imperial dread, dynasty awakening, fatal succession machine, cold title-sequence grandeur.

Timing:
- 0.0-1.2s: start low beside the huge throne-gear; paper breathes; black canal pulls the eye forward.
- 1.2-2.8s: camera glides along the canal; foreground gear rotates and slips behind the camera; ink flows ahead.
- 2.8-4.4s: pass the first raised castle and bridge-rib cluster; cinnabar node pulses as the camera moves past it.
- 4.4-6.0s: sea channels open as pale flying-white gaps; mineral-green stains bleed through selected routes.
- 6.0-7.6s: crane upward while still travelling forward; dragon-bone arcs unfold across the path.
- 7.6-9.0s: reach the high oblique aerial reveal; the full power map appears and the black dragon eclipse sweeps over it.

Audio: sound effects and ambience only; no music, no BGM, no soundtrack. Use dry brush scrape, paper fiber rumble, low gear grind, ink flow hiss, distant dragon breath, stone rise, and one deep seal-stamp thud at 8.8s.

Negative: no title text, no readable map labels, no official sigils, no photoreal metal, no glossy 3D render look, no puppet/cut-paper/leather-puppet style, no hard cut, no random camera shake.
