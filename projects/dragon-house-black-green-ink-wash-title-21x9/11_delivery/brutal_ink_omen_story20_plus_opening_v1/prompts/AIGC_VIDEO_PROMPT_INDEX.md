## Opening 9s One-Take

Images:
- Start: `images/opening_9s/opening_9s_start_brutal_ink_power_machine_21x9.png`
- End: `images/opening_9s/opening_9s_end_brutal_ink_power_machine_21x9.png`

Prompt:
Create a 9-second 21:9 one-take image-to-video shot using the start and end frames. The feeling is inspired by a fantasy title-sequence power machine, but transformed into brutal Chinese ink: a dormant rice-paper bloodline mechanism wakes, black ink channels open, crown gears rise, abstract castle blocks lift from the paper, and a vast black dragon omen closes over the completed dynasty map. Do not copy any official title design or readable map.

景别 / Shot size: macro-oblique extreme wide over a mechanical bloodline map, with strong foreground-to-background depth.

机位 / Camera position: low oblique camera skimming above old paper, starting near the foreground throne-gear seal and looking toward distant abstract castle nodes.

相机移动 / Camera movement: one continuous slow forward glide plus a gentle clockwise orbit. No cuts. Start low and close to the dormant throne-gear; by the end, rise slightly so the full activated power map and overhead dragon shadow are visible.

光影 / Lighting: pale rice-paper base light; black ink channels darken as they activate; mineral-green stains glow weakly inside selected grooves; cinnabar seal nodes pulse once; antique-gold cracks flicker along raised gears.

动作 / Action: dormant ink grooves begin flat, then lift into 3D relief. Bloodline channels fill with black ink. Tiny castle blocks rise in sequence. Crown mechanisms rotate slowly. Dragon-bone arcs unfold. At the end, a huge dry-brush dragon omen curves over the top half like an ink eclipse and presses down over the map.

人物/表情 / Character and expression: no human faces. Power is shown through mechanisms, castles, crown gears, and dragon shadow.

情绪 / Emotion: imperial dread, dynasty awakening, fatal succession machine, cold title-sequence grandeur.

Timing:
- 0.0-1.5s: old paper breathes; dormant throne-gear seal and bloodline grooves are visible but flat.
- 1.5-3.0s: black ink begins flowing through grooves; the first crown gear rotates and rises.
- 3.0-4.8s: distant castle blocks lift from paper; cinnabar nodes pulse; mineral-green poison light appears in selected channels.
- 4.8-6.6s: dragon-bone arcs unfold across the map; camera rises slightly while still gliding forward.
- 6.6-8.2s: black dry-brush dragon omen forms overhead; the central throne gear opens into a dark crown pit.
- 8.2-9.0s: settle on the final end-frame composition: completed brutal ink power machine under a dragon eclipse.

Audio: sound effects and ambience only; no music, no BGM, no soundtrack. Use dry brush scrape, paper fiber rumble, low gear grind, ink flow hiss, distant dragon breath, stone rise, and one deep seal-stamp thud at 8.8s.

Negative: no title text, no readable map labels, no official sigils, no photoreal metal, no glossy 3D render look, no puppet/cut-paper/leather-puppet style, no hard cut, no random camera shake.
