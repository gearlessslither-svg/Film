# Global Continuity Locks

Project: `dragon-house-black-green-ink-wash-title-21x9`

## Factions

- Black faction: dense black ink, dry-brush crown strokes, heavy palace silhouettes, cold gray mist.
- Green faction: mineral green / dark jade ink wash, softer but spreading forms, green banners and crown washes.
- Conflict line: a black/green ink river or bloodline crack splitting the rice-paper world.

## Dragons

Dragons are huge ink silhouettes and brush-stroke afterimages. They can appear as:

- one-stroke serpentine ink dragon
- wash bloom wing or skull shape
- split black/green dragon shadows
- fading burnt ink afterimage

Never render realistic dragon skin, muscles, scales, or photoreal fantasy creatures.

## Power Symbols

- Crown: broken ink-and-gold-leaf suggestion, not a detailed metal prop unless used as a large brush silhouette.
- Throne: empty silhouette in mist or negative space.
- Palace: layered ink mountains/court eaves; not literal photoreal architecture.
- War: banners, fleets, armies, blades, and bridges as abstract brush masses.

## Motion Language

For AIGC video prompts, movement should come from brush and paper:

- scroll unrolling
- ink bleeding through fibers
- dry brush dragging across rice paper
- wash layers separating like mist
- silhouettes forming from ink bloom and dissolving into water
- cinnabar dots or seals blooming, never readable text
