# Topic Selection - Ink-Wash Title Variant

Project: `dragon-house-black-green-ink-wash-title-21x9`
Date: 2026-07-03

## Direction

Proceed with a new 21:9 brutal splashed-ink dragon-omen variant using the same black-vs-green royal civil-war content. Visual style must come only from `04_lookdev/style_references/brutal_ink_omen_v1/director_reference_only_style.png`.

## Evaluation

- Traffic/audience potential: medium-high. The underlying royal civil-war / dragon-house premise is broadly legible, and the brutal ink dragon-omen style is more iconic and immediately readable than the earlier scenic ink-wash branch.
- Aesthetic value: high. The target uses one overwhelming black dragon gesture, negative space, cold full-body figure symbols, sparse mineral green, sparse cinnabar, old paper, and lethal restraint.
- Production difficulty: medium-high. Main risk is muddy AI texture, weak shape readability, and accidental drift into forbidden old experiments. Use the image-quality-guard generation gate and keep one overwhelming ink-dragon gesture plus one isolated subject.

## Relevant Lessons

- Establish global style and continuity locks before batch generation.
- Use motion/video reference for complex one-take camera logic, but do not expect static prompts alone to guarantee continuity.
- Keep target-style generated keyframes separate from references.
- Treat text/logos/readable symbols as high-risk; keep environment/story keyframes clean.

## Decision

Proceed. The user explicitly requested a new project and approved the water-ink xieyi direction.
