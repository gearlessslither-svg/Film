## Core Look

Use Chinese ink wash and large-brush xieyi language rather than shadow-puppet mechanics:

- black ink, diluted gray washes, dry-brush texture, flying-white brush gaps
- mineral green and dark jade washes for the green faction
- cinnabar red seals/bloodline accents, but no readable seal text
- rice-paper / xuan-paper grain, empty negative space, misty voids
- palace silhouettes, banners, crowns, thrones, dragons, ships, armies as brush forms and ink blooms
- motion implied by ink spreading, wash bleeding, brush strokes pulling across paper, scroll unfolding, mist separating layers

## Composition Rules

- Keep 21:9 wide cinematic composition.
- Use bold black/green massing rather than dense decorative detail.
- Maintain foreground, midground, and background depth through ink density, wash transparency, and negative space.
- Let one large brush gesture dominate each frame.
- Avoid centered poster-card composition unless the final beat intentionally locks into a formal emblem.

## Image Quality Rules

Generation gate:

- clean high-resolution image with low noise
- controlled detail density
- crisp readable silhouettes
- smooth gradients in wash areas
- natural paper texture, not random speckle
- no muddy micro-texture, no over-sharpened halos, no fake pixel-level detail
