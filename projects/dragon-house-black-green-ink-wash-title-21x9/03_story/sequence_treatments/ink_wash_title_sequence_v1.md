## Creative Spine

This is the same black-vs-green royal civil-war title sequence reimagined as Chinese ink-wash xieyi cinema. The world is not a mechanical puppet stage; it is a living rice-paper scroll where bloodline, claim, prophecy, and war are painted into existence by brush pressure, ink bloom, negative space, and fading dragon afterimages.

## Opening 10s One-Take

0.0-2.0s: a pale rice-paper scroll fills the frame. A single black brush line cuts across it, then a mineral-green wash answers from the opposite side.

2.0-4.0s: the camera glides along the dividing ink river. Black and green washes pull apart like a split bloodline.

4.0-6.5s: palace eaves and mountain silhouettes rise out of diluted ink. A faint dragon shape appears in negative space.

6.5-8.5s: the brush river curves into crown arcs and throne emptiness. Cinnabar droplets bloom like political wounds.

8.5-10.0s: the shot settles into a compressed omen: split black/green crown marks, empty throne silhouette, crossed ink-dragon afterimages.

## Full 14-Beat Sequence Map

1. Bloodline opens: black and green ink river splits a genealogy scroll.
2. Disputed queen: large cropped queen-mask silhouette as one black brush mass, dragon wash behind palace eaves.
3. Rogue prince: diagonal corridor strokes, blade-like sleeve, red ink dragon stroke.
4. Green court: long council table as a dark wash, green mist, empty throne negative space.
5. Coronation omen: crooked crown brush arc, small ruler silhouette, broken sun-dragon halo in cinnabar/gold wash.
6. War curse: ancient dragon skull suggested by one vast black wash over a tiny warrior.
7. Naval power: fleets and sea-serpent prow formed by ink waves and dry-brush rigging.
8. Young heirs: two small silhouettes split by torn white negative space, small dragon wisps above.
9. Dream prophecy: moth-like ink flecks orbit a pale chamber; dragon wing emerges from diluted wash.
10. Martyr rupture: red-gold sun disc fractures into wet ink cracks around a central bust.
11. Political cage: giant brush hands, seal blocks, and document strips close around a tiny figure.
12. Oath to war: low blade stroke, commander silhouette, banners and armies as horizontal ink masses.
13. Dragon war tableau: black and green armies collide under crossing ink dragons.
14. Final story aftermath: broken crown strokes, empty throne, snapped bloodline washes, fading dragon afterimages on ruined paper.

## Continuity Locks

- No text, labels, logos, watermarks, map labels, or readable seal characters.
- Use large expressive ink masses; do not fill the entire frame with noisy detail.
- Dragons are ink-brush silhouettes or wash afterimages.
- The palette is black ink, gray wash, rice-paper amber, mineral green, small cinnabar, restrained antique gold.
