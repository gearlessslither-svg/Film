# 点子发动机评分规则

Use this reference when ranking many ideas or explaining why one idea should be selected.

## Scores

Score each dimension from 1-5.

- **Chinese audience pull:** recognizability, nostalgia, social sharing potential, topic familiarity.
- **AIGC advantage:** whether the idea becomes more interesting because it is generated, transformed, stylized, impossible to shoot, or visually surprising.
- **Safety / generation risk:** low policy friction, low celebrity/IP dependence, video-model feasibility.
- **Visual novelty:** strong visual system, not just generic cinematic output.
- **Narrative clarity:** viewers can understand the hook quickly.
- **Production cost:** lower score means many assets, hard continuity, or difficult video motion.
- **Music/edit upside:** optional; score only as a bonus, never a veto.

## Risk Grades

- **Low:** archetypal characters, public-domain or generic concepts, simple motion, no exact brands/faces/text.
- **Medium:** recognizable public-domain story with sensitive depiction risk, style-homage close to known IP, multiple recurring characters, complex transitions.
- **High:** living celebrity likeness, exact copyrighted characters, real political/public-figure claims, graphic violence, precise text/logo reproduction, complex crowd/action continuity.

## Duration And Image Count

Use duration as a creative decision.

- **10-20 seconds:** 3-6 images. One punchline, one transformation, one visual loop, one memeable concept.
- **20-45 seconds:** 6-12 images. Setup, turn, payoff.
- **45-90 seconds:** 12-24 images. Trailer, short story, music-driven montage.
- **90 seconds-3 minutes:** 24-60+ images. Multi-act MV or narrative with continuity anchors.

Adjust image count:

- Add 20-40% for action, transformations, or complex spatial logic.
- Add dedicated reference/asset images when character, prop, or location continuity matters.
- Reduce count for slow ambient pieces or single-location mood videos.

## Recommendation Rule

Recommend the idea with the best balance of traffic pull, AIGC advantage, and generation safety. A slightly smaller idea that can actually be generated is better than a huge idea that will stall.
