# SCN_ARCADE Opening 15s Long Take v001

Shot ID: `LTK_ARCADE_OPEN_001`

Purpose: scene opening long take before the arcade-room sequence. Establish the place, the three brothers, and the mood before they enter the hidden arcade.

Duration target: about 15 seconds.

Aspect: 16:9 landscape.

Continuity stage: `S1_ARCADE_PLAY` entry moment, still clean-opening character state.

## Core Idea

The camera slowly sweeps from left to right across a broken old factory-side street and finds the hidden arcade entrance. At first the frame is almost empty: wet concrete, peeling walls, pipes, old wires, distant CRT hum leaking through dirty plastic strips. As the camera keeps drifting right, the three brothers enter from the right edge. They are not excited yet; they are cautious, conspiratorial, and a little afraid of being discovered. A Lei leads, Xiao Chuan follows with both hands on the backpack straps, Xiao Man is now a small chubby boy tucked close behind them, clutching his vest hem. The three slip through the plastic curtain into the arcade glow.

## Blocking

- A Lei leads by half a step. He is taller and more angular, old thin-frame glasses always visible, navy track jacket with red-white diagonal stripe.
- Xiao Chuan is in the middle. He is skinny and alert, both hands on pale green backpack straps, red scarf visible but not heroically bright.
- Xiao Man is last and closest to Xiao Chuan. He must read as a small chubby boy: round face, short round hair, warm padded vest, loose patched trousers, inward toes, clutching vest hem.
- The three should move carefully, not like comic thieves. Their sneakiness is childhood fear plus forbidden curiosity.

## Camera

- Lens: 24-28mm equivalent.
- Height: child-height, about 0.9-1.05m.
- Movement: slow lateral truck/pan left-to-right, tiny handheld drift only.
- No cuts, no fast zoom, no drone-like float.
- The final second can use the dirty plastic curtain as a natural wipe into the existing arcade entrance sequence.

## Environment Anchors

- Broken old factory-side street / back lane, wet concrete, peeling gray wall, exposed pipes, sagging wires.
- Hidden game-room entrance with no modern signage; if there is a sign, it must be unreadable or cropped.
- Dirty translucent plastic strips at the doorway.
- Dim yellow bulb at entrance.
- Interior CRT blue-green-red glow leaking out.
- Inside, preserve the SCN_ARCADE DNA: low ceiling, left/right bulky CRT cabinet rows, stools, rear fictional two-player cabinet.

As the camera continues right, it reveals a hidden old arcade entrance with dirty translucent plastic strips, dim yellow bulb, and blue-green-red CRT light leaking from inside. Keep the arcade interior consistent with the SCN_ARCADE mother image: low stained ceiling, two cramped rows of bulky CRT cabinets, old stools, peeling walls, warm bulb, fan and sagging wires, fictional 1990s arcade screen glow only.

## Negative Prompt

No modern arcade, no LCD screens, no clean mall game center, no cyberpunk neon club, no readable sign text, no real game titles or logos, no recognizable copyrighted game characters, no comedy sneaking, no exaggerated thief acting, no adult gangsters in this shot, no girls, no extra crowd, no anime, no toy-like 3D, no glamorous lighting, no subtitles, no watermark, no captions, no cuts, no sudden zoom.
