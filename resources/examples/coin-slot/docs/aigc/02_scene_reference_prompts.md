# 投币口 / 01_AIGC 场景参考图提示词 v1

这些提示词用于在生成视频前先生成场景主参考图。每个视频镜头都应继承对应场景参考图和 Blender 白模机位。

## SCENE_COMPOUND / 老旧小区偏僻角落

中文提示词：90年代中国小城非常老旧的居民小区，一楼偏僻角落里藏着一个游戏机房，像居民楼底层储物间改出来的小房子，发黄水泥墙、旧楼道、铁栏杆、杂乱电线、晾衣绳、破自行车、潮湿地面、墙角杂草。三个男孩从院子深处走向游戏机房：一个稍大一点的哥哥走在前面，主角和更小的弟弟跟在后面，没有女生。中式梦核，潮湿发黄的童年记忆，真实电影质感。

English prompt: a very old 1990s small-town Chinese residential compound, a hidden arcade room tucked into a remote ground-floor corner like a converted storage room, yellowed concrete walls, old stairwell, iron railings, tangled wires, clotheslines, broken bicycle, damp ground, weeds in corners. Three boys walk from the courtyard toward the arcade room: a slightly older brother-like boy leads, the protagonist and a smaller younger brother follow, no girls. Chinese dreamcore, humid yellowed childhood memory, cinematic realism.

## SCENE_ARCADE / 90年代小城游戏厅

中文提示词：90年代中国小城游戏厅，藏在老旧小区一楼偏僻角落，低矮天花板，左右两侧排列旧街机，核心机器是一台街头霸王街机，CRT 屏幕发出红蓝绿色荧光，空气里有烟雾和灰尘，磨损的摇杆和彩色按钮，墙上褪色游戏海报，入口处脏塑料门帘，三个男孩围在街霸机器前，没有女生，儿童低视角，中式梦核，潮湿发黄的童年记忆，电影感，真实材质，低照度，轻微录像带噪点。

English prompt: 1990s small-town Chinese arcade hall hidden in a remote ground-floor corner of an old residential compound, low ceiling, rows of old arcade cabinets on both sides, the hero cabinet is Street Fighter, CRT screens glowing red blue and green, smoky dusty air, worn joysticks and colored buttons, faded game posters, dirty plastic curtain near the entrance, three boys gathered around the Street Fighter cabinet, no girls, child-height perspective, Chinese dreamcore, humid yellowed childhood memory, cinematic realism, low light, subtle VHS noise.

## SCENE_ARCADE_DUEL / 街霸对战与找茬

中文提示词：90年代中国小城旧游戏厅内部，一台街头霸王街机前，稍大一点的哥哥正在和小矮个混混老大对战，哥哥打赢后屏幕亮起胜利画面，小矮个老大表情凶狠丢脸，旁边有瘦高个、胖子、小跑腿三个混混聚拢但暂时不动手，主角和更小的弟弟站在哥哥身后。CRT 光、烟雾、低天花板、压迫构图，真实电影感。

English prompt: inside a 1990s small-town Chinese arcade, in front of a Street Fighter cabinet, the slightly older brother has just beaten the short fierce bully boss in a match, the screen glows with a victory moment, the short boss looks humiliated and angry, a lanky tall bully, a fat bully, and a young errand-runner bully gather around but do not attack yet, the protagonist and smaller younger brother stand behind the older brother. CRT glow, smoke, low ceiling, oppressive framing, cinematic realism.

## SCENE_ALLEY / 偏僻小路堵截

中文提示词：90年代中国小城老旧小区背后的偏僻小路，发黄水泥围墙，潮湿地面，墙角杂草，碎砖石，旧路灯发出昏黄光，远处有居民楼窗户微光。稍大一点的哥哥带着主角和更小的弟弟回家，哥哥因为刚赢了街霸有点志得意满；前方小矮个混混老大带着瘦高个、胖子、小跑腿堵住小路。中式梦核，真实电影感，危险即将发生。

English prompt: a secluded back alley behind an old 1990s small-town Chinese residential compound, yellowed concrete walls, damp ground, weeds in corners, broken bricks and stones, an old streetlight casting dull yellow light, faint apartment window glow in the distance. The slightly older brother leads the protagonist and smaller younger brother home, proud after winning Street Fighter; ahead, the short fierce bully boss blocks the alley with a lanky tall bully, a fat bully, and a young errand-runner. Chinese dreamcore, cinematic realism, danger about to happen.


## SCENE_ARCADE_EXIT / 游戏机房门口离开点

中文提示词：90年代中国小城老旧小区一楼偏僻角落的游戏机房门口，脏台阶，旧卷帘门，褪色招牌，杂乱电线，昏黄路灯，门内漏出街机红蓝光。稍大一点的哥哥刚打赢街霸，志得意满地带着主角和更小的弟弟离开，三人全是男孩；远处小路更暗，隐约有不安全感，但此刻还没有真正打斗。儿童低机位，潮湿夜色，中式梦核，写实电影感。

English prompt: outside the hidden ground-floor arcade room in a remote corner of an old 1990s small-town Chinese residential compound, dirty steps, old rolling shutter, faded signboard, tangled wires, yellow sodium streetlight, red and blue arcade glow spilling from the doorway. The slightly older brother has just won Street Fighter and proudly leads the protagonist and smaller younger brother away, all three are boys; the darker alley ahead feels unsafe, but no real fight happens here yet. Child-height camera, humid night, Chinese dreamcore, cinematic realism.

## SCENE_CORRIDOR / 废楼无限走廊

中文提示词：废弃单位楼内部，狭窄低矮走廊，发霉水泥墙，脱落油漆，重复门洞，破旧消防箱，地面积水反光，冷绿色荧光灯断续闪烁，走廊仿佛无限延伸，中式梦核，安静压迫，电影感，深处黑暗。

English prompt: inside an abandoned Chinese work-unit building, narrow low corridor, moldy concrete walls, peeling paint, repeated door frames, old fire box, water puddles reflecting light, flickering cold green fluorescent tubes, corridor seems endless, Chinese dreamcore, silent oppressive atmosphere, cinematic, darkness in the distance.

## SCENE_PHONEBOOTH / 废楼内部电话亭

中文提示词：昏暗废楼走廊深处出现一个旧电话亭，电话亭不合理地发出稳定暖白微光，周围是冷绿色荧光灯和发霉墙面，小男孩从黑暗中靠近，电话铃声仿佛从远处传来，孤立、神秘、临界点，电影感。

English prompt: an old phone booth appears deep inside a dark abandoned corridor, the booth emits a steady warm white glow that feels impossible, surrounded by cold green fluorescent light and moldy walls, a small boy approaches from the darkness, the phone rings from far away, isolated mysterious threshold, cinematic.

## SCENE_8BIT / 横版街机世界

中文提示词：废楼走廊被重写成90年代横版清版街机世界，8-bit 像素风格，侧向卷轴构图，像素墙砖、像素消防箱、像素电话亭，主角变成保留蓝白校服和红领巾的小学生像素角色，追兵变成黑夹克黄发像素杂兵，屏幕上有血条和分数 UI，热血格斗式街机爽感，但底色仍然不安。

English prompt: an abandoned corridor rewritten as a 1990s side-scrolling beat-em-up arcade world, 8-bit pixel art style, side-scrolling composition, pixel wall tiles, pixel fire box, pixel phone booth, the protagonist becomes a primary-school pixel character with blue-white school jacket and red scarf, pursuers become black-jacket yellow-haired pixel enemies, health bars and score UI on screen, energetic arcade beat-em-up feeling with an uneasy undertone.
